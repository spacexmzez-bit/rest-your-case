How to Play

Core Gameplay Loop
1

Probe Your Client

In Phase 1, interview the defendant in holding. Depending on the Complexity level, your client may hide embarrassing details or outright fabricate alibis. Ask direct questions to lock down their story before the DA attacks it.

2

Manage Action Points (AP)

In Phase 2, spend AP strategically with Investigator Diaz. Subpoena cell tower dumps (/subpoena), track down missing alibi witnesses, or file motions to suppress illegal police searches.

3

Dismantle the State's Case

In Phase 3, interrupt improper questions with OBJECTION: [Grounds] (frivolous objections earn Judicial Strikes). On cross-examination, confront state witnesses with conflicting discovery exhibits to expose lies.

4

Win & Decode Truth

Deliver your closing argument highlighting reasonable doubt. After the jury returns a verdict, take the sealed Base64 hash from Turn 2, paste it into the Notebook's Truth Vault, and unseal what really happened.

Field Manual

Rules of Evidence & Commands

Courtroom evidentiary objections and 1-tap commands for seamless procedural execution.

Objection Cheat Sheet

During Phase 3, interrupt the ADA by typing OBJECTION: [Grounds]. Frivolous or incorrect objections earn a Judicial Strike.

Hearsay (FRE 802) Out-of-court statement

Witness repeats second-hand assertions to prove their truth.
Exceptions: Statements by the defendant, business records, excited utterances.

Leading Question (FRE 611c) Direct Exam Only

The Prosecutor suggests the desired answer to their own witness.
Warning: Leading is entirely legal on cross-examination. Never object there.

Speculation / Lack of Foundation (FRE 602/901) No Personal Knowledge

Witness makes inferences about motives or unseen events, or physical evidence is offered without a chain of custody.

Improper Character / Propensity (FRE 404b) Past Misconduct

The DA introduces prior bad acts to claim the client has criminal propensity. Inadmissible unless proving motive, intent, or plan.

Assumes Facts Not in Evidence Sneaked Premise

The question smuggles in an unproven allegation as an established fact (e.g., "Where did you hide the gun you stole?").

Argumentative / Badgering Harassment

The DA is yelling, debating, or cutting off answers rather than eliciting factual witness testimony.

How to Set Up Your Simulation

Run Rest Your Case inside Google Gemini in 4 steps.

1

Create a Gemini Gem (Recommended)

Open Gemini, open the side menu, and tap Gems Manager → New Gem. (If you are on the free tier, you can simply paste the prompt into any fresh Gemini chat).

2

Paste the System Directive

Name your Gem "Rest Your Case". In the Instructions field, paste the complete contents of SYSTEM_PROMPT.md from your repository, then save.

3

Initialize Turn 1 Configuration

In your new chat or Gem, start the engine by typing:

/start

The engine outputs the Phase 0 intake menu. Select your language, presentation style, difficulty, complexity, charge category, and account tier.

4

Sync with this Dashboard

When Turn 2 initializes with your case discovery, type /generate in Gemini. Copy the JSON block, navigate to this site's Notebook tab, click Initialize Case File, and paste it into Auto-Extract.
